# Brag Plan: MailMotion (builder cut)

**What it is:** an open-source, self-hostable builder for animated email signatures that actually render in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail. It runs in your browser, with no tracking and no account.
**Who it's for:** anyone who sends email and wants a signature that moves without breaking.
**What sets it apart:** it's engineered for email clients. It uses table HTML, stays under 10,000 characters, and keeps GIFs under 300 KB with frame 1 always complete.
**Focus (user request):** show both builders, Simple Style and Custom Style, using the user's details (Satiya Ganes, satiyaganes.sg@example.com).

## Angle
The real product doing its job, using real output. Every signature in the video comes from MailMotion's own renderer and serializer with Satiya's details. The builder UI is the app's real captured markup and CSS, animated. The polish comes from the motion.

## Tone
`default`, punchy and clean, with extra polish: soft staggered transitions, a camera that zooms into the action, and nothing on screen that doesn't earn its place.

## Format / duration
Landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 24 s. Frames are captured at 2× and supersampled down.

## Storyboard (times in s; music: happy-beats vol-11, 114.8 BPM, strong cues at 1.60 / 3.70 / 5.80 / 12.65 / 17.91)
1. **Hook, 0–3.7.** "Animated email signatures / that *actually render.*" rises word by word, and the italic lands on the 1.60 cue. A Gmail-style email card floats in beside it with Satiya's Aurora signature: the ring spins and the name inks itself.
2. **Pick your style, 3.7–5.8.** The real /start page. The cursor glides to Simple Style, the card lifts, a click, and the camera zooms through it.
3. **Simple Style, 5.8–12.6.** The real Simple builder. The camera pushes in on "Your details". A click on Full name, then "Satiya Ganes" is typed and the first card updates live (the ring re-renders from YN to SG). The list scrolls through the ten ready-made designs and settles on Ink signature, which writes the name stroke by stroke. Click Copy signature and the real toast appears. Caption: *Simple Style: ten ready-made designs.*
4. **Custom Style, 12.6–17.9.** The real Custom builder with its Gmail preview. Clicks on Neon, Equalizer and Wave, with the preview, remix selects and budget meters updating to the real numbers. The camera pushes onto the meters (2,870 / 10,000 chars, GIFs well under 300 KB). Captions: *Custom Style: six designs, every field* and then *Checked against Gmail's limits.*
5. **Works everywhere, 17.9–20.5.** "Works in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail." (landing copy) with the Wave signature in an email card. Three client chips pop in on the beat.
6. **Outro, 20.5–24.2.** The logo mark wipes in, then the wordmark, "Open source · self-hosted · no tracking", the "Build my signature" button, and the repo URL.
